Brightside started as a shared document. Our first team was spread from Vancouver to Manila, and the daily stand-up meant someone was always on a call at an unkind hour. So we wrote our updates down instead, and kept doing it because it worked better.

## What we believe

- **Writing beats talking for status.** People can read at their own time, and the record stays.
- **Meetings are for decisions.** If nothing needs deciding, nobody needs to meet.
- **Wellbeing is a team metric.** How people feel shows up in the work, long before it shows up in a review.

## Who we are

We are six people in four countries: engineers, a designer and someone who answers every support email. We use Brightside every day to build Brightside.
