When your team covers most of the globe, there is no good time for a meeting. There are only less bad ones. Here is the routine that works for us.

## Hand over at the end of your day

The last thing each person writes is a short handover: what they finished, and what someone in the next time zone could pick up. It turns the time difference into a relay instead of a delay.

## Keep one shared meeting a week

We kept a single weekly call, and we rotate its time so the early start is shared fairly. It is for decisions and for seeing each other's faces, never for status.

## Write decisions down where people look

A decision made in a call does not exist for the people who were asleep. We write every decision in the channel the team already reads, with the reason in one sentence.

## Protect the overlap

Most teams have one or two hours when everyone is awake. Keep them free of meetings and use them for quick questions, so the rest of the day can be deep work.
