Every morning at 9:30 Lisbon time, eight people joined a call. For three of them it was 4:30 in the morning or past dinner. The call took fifteen minutes and most of it was people reading out lists.

So we tried something simple: **write it down instead**.

## What we changed

Each person answers three questions when their day starts:

1. What did you finish yesterday?
2. What are you doing today?
3. Is anything in your way?

The answers are collected into one digest, and anything in the third answer goes to the top.

<Callout type="tip" title="Keep the questions short">
  Three questions is the limit. Every extra question we tried made people skip the check-in altogether.
</Callout>

## What we noticed after a month

- Blockers surfaced **earlier**, because writing "I'm stuck on the export" feels smaller than saying it on a call.
- People in far time zones stopped feeling like an afterthought.
- We got back about an hour of focus a week, per person.

<Callout type="note">
  We still meet. We just meet to *decide* things, not to report status.
</Callout>

## Try it yourself

You don't need Brightside to try this. Start with a shared document and the three questions above for one week. If it works for your team, [join the waitlist](/#waitlist) and we will make it easier.
